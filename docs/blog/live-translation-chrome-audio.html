Live Translation for Chrome Audio

Foreign-language videos and meetings are easier to follow when transcription and translation happen while the audio is still playing.

Updated May 10, 20264 minute read

Language learners, students, and global teams often need more than a transcript. They need a way to understand spoken content while it is happening.

Common use cases

  • Online courses taught in another language.
  • YouTube lectures, product demos, and interviews.
  • Webinars and podcasts with specialized vocabulary.
  • International meetings in Google Meet or Zoom web.

Why Chrome tab audio matters

Audicap captures the audio playing in the current Chrome tab. That means the workflow is not limited to one meeting app. If the content plays in a regular Chrome tab, Audicap can often help turn it into live text and translation.

Recommended translation workflow

  1. Open the video, meeting, or course in Chrome.
  2. Start Audicap live transcription.
  3. Turn on translation and select your target language.
  4. Review the transcript and translation after the session.
  5. Export or share the transcript if needed.

Translation is included during live sessions
