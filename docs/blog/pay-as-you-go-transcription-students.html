Pay-As-You-Go vs Monthly Transcription Plans

If your transcription needs change week by week, a monthly plan may not be the best fit. Here is how to compare the real cost.

Updated May 10, 20265 minute read

Students often need transcription in bursts: a lecture series, exam review week, a research interview, or a foreign-language course. Paying every month for software you only use occasionally can become expensive.

When subscriptions make sense

A monthly transcription plan may be reasonable if you transcribe many hours every week, need team administration, or use meeting archives daily.

When pay-as-you-go works better

  • You only transcribe during busy academic periods.
  • You need lecture notes for a few classes, not every day.
  • You want no auto-renewal and no long-term commitment.
  • You prefer transparent minute-based usage.
