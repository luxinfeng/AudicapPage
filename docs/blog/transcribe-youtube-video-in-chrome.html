How to Transcribe YouTube Videos in Chrome

Use live transcription to turn YouTube lectures, tutorials, podcasts, interviews, and webinars into searchable notes.

Updated May 10, 20266 minute read

YouTube captions are useful, but they are not always available, accurate, translated, exportable, or easy to summarize. If you learn from long videos, a Chrome audio transcription workflow can save time.

When a YouTube transcript helps most

  • Long educational videos where you need searchable notes.
  • Foreign-language videos where live translation helps comprehension.
  • Podcast-style videos where visuals are less important than spoken content.
  • Creator workflows where you need a subtitle draft in SRT or text.

How to transcribe YouTube with Audicap

  1. Open the YouTube video in Chrome.
  2. Click Audicap in the toolbar.
  3. Start recording the current tab audio.
  4. Use live translation if the video is in another language.
  5. Export the transcript as TXT, Markdown, or SRT.

FAQ

Does Audicap download the YouTube video?

No. Audicap captures audio playing in the Chrome tab for live transcription.

Can Audicap summarize a long YouTube video?

Yes. After transcript text is available, Audicap can generate key points, action items, and decisions where applicable.
