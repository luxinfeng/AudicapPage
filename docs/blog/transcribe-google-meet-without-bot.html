How to Transcribe Google Meet Without Adding a Bot

Meeting bots are not always welcome. Here is how to think about Google Meet transcription when you want live notes from browser audio without adding another participant.

Updated May 10, 20267 minute read

Google Meet transcription usually falls into three categories: built-in captions, meeting bots, and browser-tab audio capture. Each has a different privacy profile, workflow, and cost model.

The problem with meeting bots

Bot-based meeting transcription tools can be useful for team workspaces, but they also add friction. They appear as participants, may require calendar access, and can make guests ask why a third-party recorder joined the call.

For solo notes, interviews, classes, and quick meetings, a lighter workflow is often better: capture the audio already playing in your Chrome tab and turn it into a live transcript.

Three ways to transcribe Google Meet

MethodBest forTradeoff
Google Meet captionsQuick live readingUsually not designed as a searchable transcript export workflow.
Meeting botTeam meeting archivesAdds a visible participant and may require calendar or workspace setup.
Chrome tab audio capturePersonal notes and lightweight transcriptionWorks on regular browser tabs and depends on Chrome tab capture permissions.

FAQ

Can other meeting participants see Audicap?

Audicap is a Chrome extension, not a meeting bot. It does not join the meeting as a visible participant.

Can Audicap transcribe every Chrome page?

Chrome internal pages and some restricted pages cannot be captured. Audicap is intended for regular website tabs such as Google Meet, YouTube, Zoom web, and online course pages.
