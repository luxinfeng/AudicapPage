Audicap vs Otter.ai for Chrome Audio Transcription

Otter.ai is a well-known meeting transcription tool. Audicap is built for people who want to transcribe audio playing in regular Chrome tabs without adding a meeting bot.

Updated May 10, 20266 minute read

Short answer

Use Otter.ai when you want a team meeting assistant and workspace-centered meeting notes. Use Audicap when you want lightweight Chrome tab audio transcription for YouTube, Google Meet, Zoom web, lectures, podcasts, courses, and live translation.

When Audicap is a better fit

  • You want to transcribe a YouTube lecture or online course.
  • You want Google Meet notes without adding a bot.
  • You prefer pay-as-you-go credits instead of monthly billing.
  • You need live translation while audio plays in Chrome.
  • You want SRT, TXT, or Markdown export from a browser workflow.

When a meeting assistant may be better

A meeting assistant can be a better fit for organizations that need shared meeting archives, admin workflows, and calendar-driven automation. The tradeoff is setup complexity and the visible presence of a third-party participant.

FAQ

Does Audicap replace every Otter.ai use case?

No. Audicap focuses on Chrome tab audio transcription. Team meeting workspace features may require a dedicated meeting assistant.

Can Audicap transcribe Google Meet?

Yes, when Google Meet runs in a regular Chrome tab and Chrome allows tab audio capture.
