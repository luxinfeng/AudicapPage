Audicap vs Fireflies.ai: Transcribe Without a Bot

Fireflies.ai is a powerful AI assistant for team meetings. But if you are tired of bots joining your calls and emailing everyone the notes, a local Chrome tab audio capture tool might be the alternative you need.

Updated June 28, 20265 minute read

Looking for a Fireflies.ai alternative that doesn't join the meeting? Audicap provides a completely bot-free workflow. Instead of integrating with your Google Calendar and sending a virtual participant to your Zoom or Google Meet calls, Audicap is a simple Chrome extension. It silently captures the audio coming out of your browser tab and generates a live transcript just for you.

The "Bot Fatigue" Problem

Fireflies.ai is an excellent tool for organizations that want to build a searchable knowledge base of all internal meetings. However, for freelancers, salespeople, HR professionals, or individuals doing 1-on-1 discovery calls, inviting an AI bot can create friction.

Bots show up as visible participants on the guest list. This often prompts the question: "Why is this being recorded?" Furthermore, if misconfigured, meeting bots can sometimes auto-email the meeting summary to all participants on the calendar invite—a disaster if you were taking private notes on a sensitive topic.

A Private, Bot-Free Alternative

Audicap takes a different approach: Tab Audio Capture.

Because it operates as a Chrome extension, it doesn't need your calendar access. It doesn't join the meeting room. It simply listens to the audio playing in your active Chrome tab (whether that's Google Meet, Zoom Web, or Microsoft Teams Web) and transcribes it locally on your screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Fireflies alternative that doesn't join the meeting?

Audicap is a Chrome extension that captures browser audio locally. It transcribes meetings without sending a visible bot to join the call, making it a great alternative to Fireflies.ai for private notes.

Does Audicap send email summaries to participants?

No. Unlike team-focused bots, Audicap keeps your transcripts and summaries strictly local to your browser. You decide if and when to share them.

Can Audicap capture my microphone?

No. By design, Audicap only captures the audio coming out of the Chrome tab (what the other people are saying). This ensures maximum privacy for your own workspace.
