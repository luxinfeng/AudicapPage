How it works

From the active Chrome tab to History in four steps.

This is the same control flow you will see inside Audicap. No course download or audio-file upload is required.

01

Open the course tab

Play the lesson, certification module, lecture, or training video in a regular desktop Chrome tab.

02

Press Start Recording

Open Audicap, sign in if prompted, and start the active tab. If Chrome requests authorization, click the Audicap toolbar icon once.

03

Follow the Live view

Keep the course playing while transcript segments appear. Use translation, timestamps, copy, or export controls when needed.

04

Stop, then open History

Press Stop Recording to save the session. Open it in History to review, add notes, mark it for later, share, summarize, or export.

Questions

Before your first lesson.

Need a policy detail? Read our privacy policy, terms, or refund policy.

Does Audicap replace the course player?

No. Keep the original course open and playing in Chrome. Audicap captures the tab audio you choose and displays the transcript alongside your learning workflow.

Do I need to download or upload the course video?

No video download or file upload is required. Start Audicap while the lesson plays in a regular Chrome tab.

Is Audicap a meeting bot?

No. Audicap does not join a class or call as another participant. It works from the Chrome tab you select.

How does pricing work?

One credit equals one minute of real-time transcription. New accounts receive 30 free minutes, and purchased credits remain valid for 30 days.
