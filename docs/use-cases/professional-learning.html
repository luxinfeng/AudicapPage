What is Audicap for professional learning? It is a Chrome extension that creates a live transcript from course or training audio playing in your browser. It is designed for information-dense lessons where learners need to follow the speaker now and keep the material for later.

A four-step learning workflow

  1. Open the course in Chrome. Use the web version of the learning platform or training portal.
  2. Start Audicap as the lesson begins. The transcript appears while the instructor is speaking.
  3. Read along without leaving the lesson. Use the transcript as a second channel when the material, accent, or terminology is difficult.
  4. Keep the lesson as text. Return to the transcript in history or export it into the notes system you already use.

What Audicap does—and what it does not do

  • It captures Chrome tab audio live. There is no need to upload the course recording.
  • It preserves a transcript for your own workflow. You decide how to organize and review it.
  • It works best as a learning companion. It does not replace the course, the instructor, or your own notes.
  • It uses pay-as-you-go credits. You do not need to maintain a monthly subscription between courses.
